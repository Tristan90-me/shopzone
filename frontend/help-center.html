How to Navigate ShopZone

A quick walkthrough of how to shop with us from start to finish.

1

Browse or Search

Use the search bar or category links to find products. You can filter by price, category and stock on the Products page.

2

View Product Details

Click any product to see its full description, price, available stock and images before deciding.

3

Add to Cart

Choose your quantity and add items to your cart. Your cart is saved automatically as you continue browsing.

4

Checkout

Enter your contact details, choose delivery or pickup, and review your order summary before placing it.

5

Track Your Order

After checkout you'll receive an order number. Use it as a reference if you ever need to contact us about your order.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer delivery and pickup?
Yes — at checkout you can choose either delivery to your address or pickup from our store. Delivery fees and availability are set by the store and shown clearly before you confirm your order.
How do I know if a product is in stock?
Every product page shows a stock badge — green means in stock, amber means low stock, and grey means it's currently unavailable. Out-of-stock items can't be added to your cart.
Can I change my order after placing it?
Once an order is placed it goes straight to processing, so changes can't be made automatically. Please contact us as soon as possible using your order number and we'll do our best to help.
What if I have a problem with my order?
Send us a message using the "Message Us" feature below with your name, phone number and order number if you have one. We'll get back to you as quickly as possible.
